This guide is for content editors who create and manage pages in Primo. You'll learn how to create pages, add blocks, edit content, and publish your work.

<Info>
If you're a developer setting up Primo, share this guide with your content editors.
</Info>

## The Editor Interface

The Primo editor has three main areas:

1. **Sidebar** - Page outline and available blocks
2. **Canvas** - Visual page editor
3. **Toolbar** - Site navigation, Pages, and Publish button

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Pages dialog">
  Click "Pages" in the toolbar.
</Step>

<Step title="Create new page">
  Click "Create Page".
</Step>

<Step title="Choose page type">
  Choose from the page types your developer configured. Their names and layouts depend on your site.

  <Info>
  Page types determine which blocks you can use. Your developer configures these.
  </Info>
</Step>

<Step title="Set page details">
  Fill in:
  - **Name**: Name shown in the page list; it appears to visitors only if your developer uses it in the design
  - **Slug**: URL path (auto-generated)
  - Content fields your developer defined, such as an article title or author
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
Your page is created. Fill in its content; if its page type allows layout changes, you can also add blocks.
</Check>

## Working with Blocks

A block is a reusable design your developer builds or imports. Each placement on a page has its own section content. The blocks available to you depend on the page type.

<Steps>

<Step title="Edit content">
  Open **Edit content** from the block toolbar and fill in the fields, then choose **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Drag blocks to reorder them:

1. Hover over a block
2. Use the drag handle (appears in toolbar)
3. Drag up or down to new position

You can also open the block actions menu in the page outline and choose **Move up** or **Move down**. Use Tab to focus the menu and Enter or Space to open it; this avoids dragging.

### Deleting Blocks

1. Hover over a block
2. Click the trash icon in the block toolbar
3. Confirm deletion

## Publishing

When the site's saved changes are ready, publish the site. Publishing includes changes on other pages too:

1. Click "Publish" in the toolbar (or press `Cmd/Ctrl + P`)
2. Click "Publish Changes" in the dialog
3. Wait for the success message; timing depends on site size and your browser

<Check>
Your changes are now live!
</Check>

<Info>
Inline edits and page layout changes auto-save. Changes in block, section, and site dialogs require **Save**. Saved changes become public when you publish the site.
</Info>

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Cmd/Ctrl + P`: Publish
- `Cmd/Ctrl + ↑/↓`: Navigate between pages
- `Cmd/Ctrl + E`: Open the hovered section editor; developers can also toggle Code and Content tabs
- `Cmd/Ctrl + S`: Save (in block/section editors)
