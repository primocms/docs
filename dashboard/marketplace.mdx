## Find a starter or block

Open **Marketplace** from the dashboard. Use its **Starters** and **Blocks** tabs and category groups to browse available designs. You need network access to the marketplace to load its catalog and previews.

## Create a site from a starter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create Site">
    From Sites, choose **Create Site** and enter a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a starter">
    In the starter picker, browse the Marketplace and select a design. Review its preview before proceeding. You can also copy an existing site or choose a blank starter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review blocks and create">
    Select any optional blocks offered by the wizard and complete creation. Open the new site from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace sample content">
    Review pages, images, links, site fields, and page types. Replace starter content and connect a domain before publishing for visitors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a marketplace block to your library

In the **Blocks** tab, select a block, review its preview, choose a destination library group, and add it to your library. Add the library copy to your site's page type when you want editors to use it.

<Note>
Starters and blocks are copied into your instance. Later marketplace changes do not automatically update those copies. Review imported code and any external scripts or services before publishing.
</Note>

## Troubleshoot a missing preview

Check your browser's network requests and connection to the marketplace. A starter can preview without its own custom domain through the marketplace's preview route. If one item fails while others work, try another starter and report the item's name in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/primocms/primo/issues).
